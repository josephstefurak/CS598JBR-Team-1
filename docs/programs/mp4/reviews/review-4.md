## 4. The `read_test_vector` ruling (step 5)

Staff ruling, 2026-11-13 (verbatim in where.md O1): implement the correct Rust behaviour corresponding to
the original C, do not modify the provided tests, document the issue in the discussion section of the
report.

Facts re-read in the sources:

- C: `read_test_vector` declares `int step = 0;` (`MP4/subjects/project_2/c/test.c:118`) and passes
  `&step` to `psbt_read` (`:125`). The checker reads it through `elem->user_data` (`:91`), checks the
  record type at steps 0 and 1 and runs `(*step)++` (`:108`). The counter persists across callbacks.
  Lines 90-128 hold both functions.
- Rust port (benchmark file, byte-identical to `subjects/`): `let mut step = 0;` inside the callback
  (`src/bin/test.rs:122`), so every record is compared with type 0 at `:126`.
- Measured: `panicked at src/bin/test.rs:126:26`, `left: 4`, `right: 0`; 3 of 4.

README: the intervention entry said "A ruling from the course staff is pending". **It did not match the
ruling; fixed** (§5, F1). It now states the ruling and both locations. The draft has the recommended
discussion paragraph in "Overall Results".

D-CREDIT is recorded as the team's assumption: 3 of 4 earns full correctness credit when justified in the
report. Staff did not say so, and did not say whether Project 2 has additional grading tests (why.md
RC3). The draft's closing list tells the writer not to present it as a staff statement.
